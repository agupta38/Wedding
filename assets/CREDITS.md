# Destination photographs

- **Valley of Flowers** — Kp.vasant, [Valley of Flowers](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Valley_of_Flowers.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
